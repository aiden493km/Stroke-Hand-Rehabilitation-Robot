# Stroke-Hand-Rehabilitation-Robot
Under the joint guidance of Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University and NUS (Suzhou) Research Institute, we designed a hand rehabilitation robot for stroke patients.

On this basis, we have achieved the following:
- Jiangsu Province Undergraduate Innovation Training Project
- 2025 China Robotics and Artificial Intelligence Competition-Innovation Competition
- 2025 XJTLU Undergraduate Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition
